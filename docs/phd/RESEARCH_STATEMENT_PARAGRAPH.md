# Research statement paragraph

> SynthAudit estimates representation validity, corpus novelty and evidence-based plausibility. It does not establish experimental feasibility, yield, selectivity, safety or scalability.

My research interest is trustworthy AI for chemistry at the boundary between molecular
generation and evidence-based review. I developed SynthAudit, a representation-agnostic framework
that normalizes mapped reaction SMILES, traversal-dependent reaction-edit languages, and route
exports into a versioned atom-map-preserving intermediate representation; executes reaction-centre,
completion, and stereo operations transactionally; and keeps structural validity, corpus novelty,
precedent support, calibrated evidence, uncertainty, and route context separate. The project
combines cheminformatics, schema design, calibration, OOD detection, abstention, controlled
counterfactuals, and prompt robustness in an offline reproducible package. Its v1.0 report also
embodies the scientific practice I want to bring to doctoral research: small software fixtures are
reported only as fixture observations, while unperformed large-corpus, checkpoint, provider, and
cross-system experiments remain explicitly `not_run`. I hope to extend this platform with licensed
reaction data, adjudicated labels, parent-grouped evaluation, and prospective expert studies to
understand when AI retrosynthetic proposals are representation-consistent, evidence-supported, or
appropriately deferred for review.
